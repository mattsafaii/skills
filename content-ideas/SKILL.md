# Content Ideas Skill

Look back over a window of time, sweep **everything** Matt worked on, distill the insights, and turn them into content idea cards — each drafted (or seeded) and pointing back to where it came from.

This is the **retrospective** counterpart to `/distribute` (which expands one known project). Content Ideas' hard part is the **sweep + distillation across many sources** and finding the cross-project patterns a single dev-log session can't see.

Open `@references/guide.md` and follow it. Do not proceed without it.
