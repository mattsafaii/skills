# Case law — marks and objects this office has put on the record

Exhibits, not new principles. When critiquing or proposing, name one of these if it fits. Load with `logo-doctrine.md`.

## Do not fix what is not broken

**Eaton.** Logo changed only when the company name changed. Current mark since the 1970s, still going. “Nothing is broken.”

Rule: a refresh needs a reason other than the calendar. Name change, merger with a real identity problem, or a mark that fails SNR. Boredom at the brand committee is not a reason.

**Cognex (1981–).** Unchanged since founding. Eurostile Extended Bold on yellow. Name from “Cognition Experts.” Product names that are actual names: DataMan, VisionPro, In-Sight, DataMatrix. Machine-vision company whose mark looks like a machine-vision company. QA on Coke cans, Amazon packages, iPhone chips.

Rule: industrial yellow + a hard extended grotesque + a name that means something will outlast a circle-and-wordmark kit.

## Domain symbols and schematic marks

**RAMTRON F-RAM.** Peak lockup. Crystal/cell diamond + custom wordmark + device line. See `worked-examples.md`.

**Capp Inc. / hydraulics-adjacent industrials.** “Peak logo galore.” An old mark that reads like a **hydraulics schematic symbol** is a compliment. Construction visible. Niche symbology.

**NASA worm.** “Hyperfuturistic in the 70s and will always be.” Do not treat revival of a strong 1970s mark as nostalgia cosplay.

**Western Acoustics.** An actual USGC consignment (logo, brand name, type) with Acme SF. Office has since stopped taking consignments. Proof they will ship a full identity, not only type.

## Naming is branding

**Sun / UltraSPARC II, codename A23-Deuterium, Ultra 60 workstation.** “Absolute apex in branding. Unrepentantly glorious. Your product names will never be this cool.”

Rule: part numbers, codenames, and product lines are identity. `TX-02`, `FM18L08-70-SG`, `RETICLE 5201-010` sit in this family. Do not replace a good part name with “Pro” and a colorway.

## Utilitarian identity

**Charles Schwab.** History, products, philosophy, service, attention to detail. Hold music is a man reading today’s market — not jazz, not a jingle. “Utilitarian and respects your time.” Ads that used SF counterculture without turning the mark into a lifestyle brand.

Rule: the brand includes the hold queue, the spine, the crockery. Not only the .svg.

## Peak window, stated plainly

**“Circa 1975, we peaked.”** Cited against a *Design Concept Realisation* ABC-edition object: dead manufacturing, EOL parts, no touchscreen, “an absolute joy to operate.”

**1960s–80s graphic design** as the peak reference window (Rand on Ford, 1965). Adopt what is good.

**Aspirational object:** design the 1996 Initech annual report and shareholder letter. Corporate kitsch with a straight face.

**DEC** as a theme worth cooking — Digital Equipment Corporation as a complete visual world, not a sticker.

## Conformity and DIY

In an age of monotony, uniqueness is the primary objective of a visual identity. DIY marks go hard *because* they do not look like the global kit — even when they are clumsy. “Having no design knowledge is a prerequisite” was half a joke and half a diagnosis of Design Communism.

Do not use that as permission to be sloppy. Use it as permission to be specific.

## Bank Gothic / control-tower / instrument

Bank Gothic through a document: noted as interesting, in-register. Control-tower detailing: in-register. Instrument / VFD / barcode-printer logic: in-register. These are cousins of Berkeley Mono, not a separate style pack.
