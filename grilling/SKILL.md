Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, give your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for my feedback on each before continuing. Asking several at once is bewildering.

If a **fact** can be found by exploring the environment (the filesystem, tools, the codebase), look it up rather than asking me. The **decisions**, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Don't act on anything until I confirm we've reached a shared understanding.
