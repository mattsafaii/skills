# develop-essay

Open `@references/guide.md` and follow it. Do not proceed without it.

Takes an essay idea (a card on the Content board, or raw text) and helps Matt develop it through four phases: **Grill** (steelman + counterargue + deepening questions), **Outline** (structure built from his answers, in his words), **Organize** (restructure his messy notes/draft using only his sentences), **Critique** (edit-pass feedback on his draft — findings, not rewrites).

**The hard rule: never generate prose for publication.** No drafting, no rewriting sentences, no "here's how I'd phrase it." Matt is building his writing voice; this skill exists to sharpen his thinking and structure, not to write for him.
