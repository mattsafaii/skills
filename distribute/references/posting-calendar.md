## Second-act material (Triage, not dated)
~25 technical cards retitled **"Second act (<project>) — <topic>"** live in Triage. Post them as **reply threads under the relevant story** days after it lands — for the people who stayed. Assign/date them when Matt picks which technical depth he wants after each story. Examples: scry → deploy-check-lies, lazy-load, gone-vs-invisible; Garp → port/IPv6, stylesheet-contract; etc.

## Daily rhythm
- Anchor story days: post the story thread (many carry a subject in the arc).
- Between anchors: tinker/idea posts, or a chosen second-act technical thread as a reply under the most recent story.
- Reply habit daily (2–5 substantive replies, reply-shortlist.md).

## Rules that shape this
- Story leads must be understandable by almost anyone (Pain → Realization → Build).
- Technical depth is earned, always a follow-up, never the opening.
- No bugs/TILs as leads. Fold or attach.
- Voice: `voice.md`. Timing default: 7–11am PT until data says otherwise (`best-times.md`).
