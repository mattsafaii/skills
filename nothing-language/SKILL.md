# Nothing-language

Write sentences a stranger can repeat. This skill is for paragraphs. For marks and layouts use `us-graphics`. For specification UI verbs and catalog-style names use `us-graphics-voice`.

Load-bearing text is Evan Conrad, Please just tell me what you do (`references/source.md`).

## Stance

Say what it does. Then stop.

- Be boring. A Figma plugin that generates SVG icons. Plaid for messaging apps.
- Real nouns. Visits. Form. Rank. Download. Error. The name of the object in the room.
- Mission, vision, culture, and origin story wait until the reader knows the product.
- If Emily cannot tell a coworker what you do, the sentence failed.

Two edges. Stay in the middle.

| Infantilize | Center | Costume |
|---|---|---|
| Oops! You're in! Got it! | What it does, in common words | Praxis. Commencement. Disposition. An investigation into |
| Move money. Ship it. | Transfer funds, when that is the real name. Otherwise send the payment. | Initiate the disbursement |

Refuse both. Slop on the left is not fixed by Latin on the right.

## Nothing-language — strike it

Nothing-language is describing a fund as an investigation into how we generate dispersed intimacy, signify alliance, and physical representations of our digital coordination praxis instead of saying it is an investment fund.

Also strike, then explain again

- revolutionize
- create the operating system for
- build at the intersection between
- empower, democratize, individually flourish, be interdisciplinary
- unlock, seamless, journey, delight, next-gen
- why we exist, how good we are, the family-slaying origin

before the reader knows the object.

If a sentence could describe twelve unrelated companies, it describes none.

## Baby-talk — also strike it

Do not swing the other way.

- Error, not Oops.
- Registration complete, not You're in.
- Download, not Get it now.
- Cancel, not Never mind.

Helpful text is useful. It is not cute. It is not a grin on a failure.

## Engineering terms — keep the trade, drop the framework's

The jargon test: would a developer understand this word on sight?

Keep standard engineering vocabulary. Scaffold. Source of truth. Failure modes — sharper than common mistakes. Do not round a term down; the target is the word a developer would use, not the simplest one.

Translate framework-internal terms at the boundary. Exemplar becomes example. Encode the convention becomes follow the convention. A skill's terms of art stay inside the skill; they never reach chat or a doc the reader gets.

## How to edit

1. Find the object. Name it.
2. Say what happens to it, in the words the operator already uses.
3. Cut mission, mood, and costume.
4. Read it aloud as if to Emily at a party. If she cannot pass it on, rewrite.

Offer an edit list when the user already has copy. Do not invent theater they did not ask for. Do not put part numbers, bulletin chrome, or foundry jokes on a page that is not a foundry bulletin.

## When this is the wrong skill

- Scoring a mark or drawing a lockup → `us-graphics`
- File names, product codes, button labels that should match a spec → `us-graphics-voice`
- A USGC public-affairs bulletin they explicitly asked for → `us-graphics-voice`

A landing page can use both. Hero sentence from here. Button from voice.
