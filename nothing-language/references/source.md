Paraphrase in work. Do not paste the essay.

## Definition

Nothing-language is describing your product as "an investigation into how we generate dispersed intimacy, signify alliance, and physical representations of our digital coordination praxis" instead of saying you are an investment fund.

## Rules he actually wrote

- Please don't fill your website and pitches with nothing-language.
- If you are going to revolutionize, create the operating system for, build at the intersection between, empower, democratize, individually flourish, or be interdisciplinary, the universe should pause, rewind, and let you explain again.
- You don't need the personal backstory first. The specter of e-commerce integrations did not need to slay your family.
- You don't need mission, vision, culture, or how good you are before you say what you do.
- Be boring. "Plaid for messaging apps." "A Figma plugin that generates SVG icons from GPT-3." "Chrome extension that adds cmd-k to every website."
- Describe things someone can explain to someone else, or you miss word of mouth.
- If someone cannot understand what the product does, then it cannot fail. Critique becomes "they just don't understand."
